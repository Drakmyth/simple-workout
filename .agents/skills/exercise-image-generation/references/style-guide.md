# Exercise image style guide

## Visual system

- Photorealistic instructional fitness photography
- White or very light-gray studio background
- Soft, even clinical lighting with minimal shadows
- Gray fitted athletic clothing with restrained dark-red accents
- Neutral, focused, closed-mouth expression
- Approachable healthy build rather than bodybuilding presentation
- Realistic anatomy, hands, feet, equipment, and joint positions
- No text, arrows, labels, borders, logos, watermarks, or extra people

## Source composition

- PNG source at 1536 x 1024 pixels
- Landscape 3:2 canvas containing two equal visual halves
- Start pose entirely in the left half
- Finish pose entirely in the right half
- Clear central separation without a visible divider
- Same model, scale, camera angle, lighting, and equipment in both halves
- Complete anatomy and equipment visible in the full composition
- Balanced margins around both poses

The application presents the optimized composite directly on portrait mobile screens. Do not depend on a carousel or embedded instructional text.

## Model variants

Use the canonical `male.png` or `female.png` reference sheet. Preserve recognizable identity, hair, body proportions, clothing, and shoes. Different exercises may use different camera angles when needed to explain form.

## Prompt contents

Every prompt must state:

- Exercise name and intended beginner variation
- Canonical model reference and variant
- Exact start position
- Exact finish position
- Critical alignment and safety cues visible in the image
- Equipment type and exact count
- Camera angle needed to understand the movement
- Source dimensions and left/right composition
- Prohibited form errors specific to the exercise
- General visual-system restrictions
