# Exercise image review checklist

## Technical form

- Start and finish positions depict the intended exercise variation.
- Spine, neck, joints, grip, stance, and equipment path are plausible and safe.
- The image does not encourage a common form error.
- Range of motion is appropriate for a beginner or clearly qualified in written guidance.

## Image integrity

- The same person appears in both poses.
- Identity and clothing reasonably match the canonical reference.
- Facial expression is neutral and natural.
- Anatomy contains no missing, duplicated, merged, or distorted parts.
- Equipment count and construction are correct.
- Hands and feet interact convincingly with equipment and surfaces.

## Composition

- Source is 1536 x 1024 and landscape.
- Start pose appears clearly on the left and finish pose on the right.
- Both poses include complete relevant anatomy and equipment.
- Scale and camera angle are consistent.
- The full composition remains understandable at portrait-phone width.
- No visible divider, displaced anatomy, or center-gutter artifact remains.

## Presentation

- Background, lighting, clothing, and accents match the project style.
- No text, arrows, labels, logos, watermarks, or unintended objects appear.
- The pose remains clear at reduced mobile size.
- Alternative text can describe the visible position concisely.
